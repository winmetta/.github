# Win Metta

Sharing Theravāda Buddhist teachings and supporting learning, meditation, and monastic communities.

Win Metta is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit that preserves and shares Theravāda Buddhist teachings. We serve learners in Myanmar and around the world through Dhamma teaching, meditation retreats, scripture study, and Burmese language education, with resources in Burmese and English.

[Visit Win Metta](https://winmetta.org) · [Explore the Dhamma Library](https://winmetta.org/dhamma-library) · [Try the platform (Beta)](https://app.winmetta.org)

## What we support

- **Dhamma teaching and meditation:** classes, guided practice, and online and in-person retreats.
- **Scripture and language study:** Pāḷi and Tipiṭaka study, and Burmese literacy through programs such as _Let's Learn Burmese_.
- **Access to teachings:** a free Dhamma Library of books, recordings, and study materials.
- **Monastic communities:** technology and administrative support for teachers and monasteries.

Visit [winmetta.org](https://winmetta.org) for program information and ways to participate. This GitHub organization is home to the software and shared tools that support that work.

## How we build

- **Free access:** teaching content is never behind a paywall or a required login.
- **Respect for the teachings:** teachers provide the content; technology helps people access it.
- **Burmese and English:** Unicode Burmese and language choice are central to our work.
- **Calm design:** no ads, streaks, leaderboards, or engagement-driven distractions.
- **Simple and sustainable:** low-cost tools that volunteers can maintain.
